# Review concepts: evidence audit

Research date: **26 October 2026**.

All **61 concept lessons** were checked through three subject-domain audits, using SciSpace for discovery and original journal publications, professional guidance and established references for claim checks. The revised library cites **124 distinct reference URLs**; these include guidance and textbooks as well as research papers, not 124 independent studies. Every lesson has at least two relevant references.

## Findings

- **28 supported:** the central explanation was supported; references and wording were strengthened where useful.
- **32 qualified:** added distinctions, exceptions or contextual limits. This does not mean the whole original lesson was false.
- **1 corrected:** oxygen saturation, pulse-oximeter estimation and arterial oxygen pressure are now explicitly distinguished.

Important refinements include oxygen-measurement limitations, myocardial injury versus infarction, preserved-ejection-fraction heart failure, digoxin/electrolyte interactions, DKA potassium shifts and euglycemic presentations, IV tonicity versus bag osmolarity, B12 findings without anemia, and infiltration versus vesicant extravasation.

Representative original sources:

- [Gudelunas et al., pulse-oximetry study, 2024 issue](https://doi.org/10.1213/ANE.0000000000006755): original publisher abstract and metadata, with the healthy-volunteer/two-device limits recorded.
- [2024 adult hyperglycemic-crises consensus, Diabetes Care](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11272983/): full-text physiological and context checks for the DKA lesson.
- [Tonicity and osmolality review, Canadian Journal of Hospital Pharmacy, 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10522352/): distinction between solution labelling and physiological effect.
- [World Allergy Organization anaphylaxis guidance, 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7607509/): supports the distinction between localized allergic symptoms and anaphylaxis.

## Scope and limits

This is a targeted literature/reference cross-check, **not a systematic review, clinician sign-off, instructor approval or guarantee of universal accuracy**. The evidence matrix records full-text, abstract-only, indexed-section and official-guidance access separately. Undated or unverified publication years remain null rather than being guessed. Study populations and guideline jurisdiction limits are retained in each evidence record.

The 198 PNLE question stems, answer keys and rationales were **not** clinically revalidated. They remain unchanged. One concept mapping was corrected: periop-023 now leads to the potassium lesson rather than generic IV access. All 61 IDs remain available, and Review still shows concepts rather than answer keys. Clinical calculations, scoring, authentication and analytics functions were not changed by this audit.

Faculty/clinical review is still needed before presenting this material as clinically approved. Future guideline changes should trigger another source review.
