SciSpace discovery question: “How reliable are pulse oximetry oxygen saturation readings for detecting arterial hypoxemia, including the effects of skin pigmentation and poor perfusion?”

One relevant result was Gudelunas et al., SciSpace paper ID `1xf06ta7br`, DOI [10.1213/ANE.0000000000006755](https://doi.org/10.1213/ANE.0000000000006755). SciSpace listed 2023; the original publisher verifies the March 2024 issue, volume 138(3), pages 552–561, with acceptance in 2023. Bibliographic dates must therefore be checked against the original record rather than copied from a search index.

Access: original publisher abstract and metadata, not subscriber full text. The prospective laboratory study enrolled 146 healthy volunteers and tested two devices. It supports qualifying pulse-oximetry accuracy by pigmentation/perfusion; it does not justify universal device-performance claims, race-based correction rules, or treatment thresholds. No such thresholds are being added to the lesson.
