# Fleming 2011 pediatric heart rate and respiratory rate centiles

- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3789232/
- Journal: Lancet. 2011 Mar 19;377(9770):1011–1018. doi: 10.1016/S0140-6736(10)62226-X
- Title: Normal ranges of heart rate and respiratory rate in children from birth to 18 years of age: a systematic review of observational studies
- Access date: 2026-10-01
- Status: FETCHED (PMC manuscript plus the web appendix tables)

The paper does not declare one “normal” band. Web Tables 4 and 5 give the 1st, 10th, 25th, median, 75th, 90th, and 99th centiles so a setting can choose a cutoff. NursePath uses the outer rows, the 1st and 99th centiles, and says so on the result.
