This is the who.int page that lists the adult categories used below. The obesity fact sheet states only the overweight and obesity cuts.

Copied from the page:

- BMI <17.0: thinness
- BMI <18.5: underweight
- BMI 18.5-24.9: normal weight
- BMI ≥25.0: overweight
- BMI ≥30.0: obesity

The page also says the health risks associated with increasing BMI are continuous, and that interpretation of BMI grading in relation to risk may differ for different populations. It does not give numeric Asia-Pacific cutoffs.
