# AAFP summary of the AAP 2017 pediatric BP guideline

- URL: https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2018/0415/p543.html
- Title: Screening and Management of High BP in Children and Adolescents: An Updated Guideline from the AAP
- Edition/year: American Family Physician. 2018;97(8):543-544. It cites Pediatrics, September 2017;140(3).
- Access date: 2026-09-30
- Status: PARTIAL as a summary. The classification table is now in `aap-2017-flynn-pediatric-bp.md`, from the guideline this page cites.

Numbers that are on this AAFP page:

- “BP should be measured yearly in patients three years and older.”
- “The goal of treatment in children should be to reduce systolic and diastolic BP to less than the 90th percentile and less than 130/80 mm Hg in those 13 years and older.”
- “HTN can be diagnosed if BP is at least in the 95th percentile on auscultation measurement at three separate office visits.”
- Diabetes: treat if BP “is at least in the 95th percentile or greater than 130/80 mm Hg in patients 13 years and older.”

The percentile stage table itself is not printed on this summary page.
