# CDC child and teen BMI categories

- URL: https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/child-teen-calculator/bmi-categories.html
- Title: Child and Teen BMI Categories
- Edition/year: Page date June 28, 2024. Categories are for children and teens 2 through 19, using sex-specific BMI-for-age percentiles. The page also cites Hampl et al., Pediatrics. 2023;151(2):e2022060641, for an expanded severe-obesity definition.
- Access date: 2026-09-30
- Status: FETCHED

Table copied from “BMI categories for children and teens 2 through 19”:

```
BMI Category     BMI Range
Underweight      Less than the 5th percentile
Healthy Weight   5th percentile to less than the 85th percentile
Overweight       85th percentile to less than the 95th percentile
Obesity          95th percentile or greater
Severe Obesity   120% of the 95th percentile or greater, or 35 kg/m2 or greater
```

Expanded AAP definition on the same page:

```
Class 2 Obesity  120% to less than 140% of the 95th percentile, or BMI 35 kg/m2 to less than 40 kg/m2
Class 3 Obesity  140% of the 95th percentile or greater, or BMI 40 kg/m2 or greater
```

The page says race is not a factor in calculating BMI percentiles, and that people 20 and older should use the adult BMI calculator. It does not print adult WHO cutoffs.
