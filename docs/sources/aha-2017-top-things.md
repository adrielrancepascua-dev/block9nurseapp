# 2017 hypertension guideline, top things to know

Comparison only. This is the summary page, not the full 2017 guideline.

The page says the 2017 guideline uses an average SBP ≥130 mm Hg or an average DBP ≥80 mm Hg to designate hypertension, compared with average SBP ≥140 mm Hg or average DBP ≥90 mm Hg in recent previous guidelines.

Table copied from the page (“Categories of BP in Adults”):

```
BP Category    SBP                DBP
Normal         <120 mm Hg    and  <80 mm Hg
Elevated       120–129 mm Hg and  <80 mm Hg
Hypertension
Stage 1        130–139 mm Hg or   80–89 mm Hg
Stage 2        ≥140 mm Hg    or   ≥90 mm Hg
```

Footnote on the page: individuals with SBP and DBP in 2 categories should be designated to the higher BP category. BP is based on an average of ≥2 careful readings obtained on ≥2 occasions.

Crisis (≥180 or ≥120): NOT FOUND as a number. The page mentions hypertensive crisis or emergency with no mm Hg cutoffs.

Low BP (<90 or <60): NOT FOUND.

Pregnancy BP numbers: NOT FOUND. The page says management may be modified for women, including those who are pregnant, and gives no pregnancy BP table.
